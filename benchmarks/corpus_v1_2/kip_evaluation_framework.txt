The KIP evaluation harness provides deterministic mechanical measurement of retrieval and RAG stages.
Core schemas defined in evaluation.models include BenchmarkCase, BenchmarkDataset, RetrievalMetrics, and BenchmarkResult.
Retrieval Hit Rate and Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) are computed exclusively across labelled cases.
Cases with empty expected_document_ids are tracked as unlabelled and excluded from Hit Rate calculation.
This design prevents empty ground-truth arrays from artificially inflating Hit Rate to 1.0.
Infrastructure failures during search are captured in retrieval_error, distinguishing outages from genuine misses.
The runner executes against direct Python service interfaces rather than HTTP to eliminate network latency noise.
