Alternative document chunking strategies include semantic chunking and recursive character splitting.
Semantic chunkers analyze sentence embeddings to detect natural topic transitions before splitting text.
Recursive chunkers split text hierarchically using double newlines, single newlines, and spaces.
Sentence-window chunking attaches surrounding sentences as contextual metadata around small central chunks.
Document-specific chunkers parse markdown headers (#, ##) or HTML tags to preserve section boundaries.
These methods aim to preserve semantic cohesion at the cost of non-deterministic chunk boundaries.
KIP currently uses a fixed character chunker; alternative chunkers are candidates for v1.2 research.
Evaluating chunking strategies requires measuring Hit Rate and MRR across varying chunk sizes and overlaps.
