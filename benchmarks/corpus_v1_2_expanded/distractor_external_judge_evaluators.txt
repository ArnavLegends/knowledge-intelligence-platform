Automated RAG evaluation frameworks such as Ragas, DeepEval, and TruLens use LLMs as evaluative judges.
These frameworks measure semantic metrics: faithfulness, answer relevance, context precision, and recall.
Faithfulness measures whether all claims in a generated answer are grounded in the retrieved context.
Context precision evaluates whether retrieved chunks are sorted with the most relevant passages first.
LLM-as-judge evaluation introduces non-determinism, cost, and reliance on powerful judge models like GPT-4.
KIP v1.1 deliberately avoids external LLM judge frameworks, relying on mechanical evaluation in runner.py.
Mechanical metrics (Hit Rate, MRR, keyword coverage) are deterministic, fast, and require no judge API quota.
Integration with Ragas or DeepEval is planned as an optional evaluation mode for future research versions.
