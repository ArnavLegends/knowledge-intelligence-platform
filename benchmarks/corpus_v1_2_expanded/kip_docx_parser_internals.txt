The DOCX parser implementation in KIP extracts textual content from Microsoft Word OpenXML documents.
Uploaded byte streams are parsed in memory using docx.Document without requiring local disk serialization.
The parser iterates over document.paragraphs, extracting clean text while skipping empty whitespace lines.
To capture structured tabular information, the parser loops through document.tables and cell rows.
Table cells are joined with pipe delimiters to preserve tabular column relationships within the text stream.
Headers and footers are filtered out to prevent repetitive boilerplate from polluting embedding representations.
Special XML formatting artifacts and embedded images are safely ignored during paragraph extraction.
The combined paragraph and table text is returned as a single normalized string for downstream chunking.
