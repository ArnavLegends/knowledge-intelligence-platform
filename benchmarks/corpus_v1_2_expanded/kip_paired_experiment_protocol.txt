KIP evaluates retrieval optimizations using a rigorous paired comparative experimentation protocol.
Every experiment defines a Control condition (existing baseline) and a Treatment condition (optimization candidate).
Both conditions are evaluated against the exact same corpus snapshot, query set, and ground-truth labels.
To prevent vector contamination, Control and Treatment are indexed into separate dedicated Qdrant collections.
The runner evaluates queries in identical order and computes paired differences (delta RR, delta latency).
Results record per-query rank shifts to determine whether aggregate gains mask individual case regressions.
Timestamped result JSON files are persisted in benchmarks/ for verifiable, peer-reviewable research records.
Optimizations are adopted into production only when empirical paired improvements outweigh regressions.
