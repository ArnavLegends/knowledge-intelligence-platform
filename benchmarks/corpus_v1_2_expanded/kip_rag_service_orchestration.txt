RAGService coordinates the end-to-end question answering pipeline across KIP subsystem components.
Upon receiving a RAGRequest, RAGService extracts query text, workspace_id, and retrieval top_k.
Step 1: It invokes RetrievalService.search() with a workspace-scoped RetrievalQuery entity.
Step 2: If chunks are retrieved, it formats the context block into ordered [Source X] XML sections.
Step 3: It constructs the grounded RAG prompt and passes it to LLMService for generative completion.
Step 4: It parses the LLM output, pairs source references with retrieved chunks, and records latencies.
Step 5: It packages the final output into a RAGResponse entity containing answer, sources, and metadata.
Any unexpected exception during retrieval or generation is caught and wrapped into a RAGError entity.
