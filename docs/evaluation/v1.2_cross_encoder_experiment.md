## 1. Executive Summary

In accordance with the failure analysis findings in [`docs/evaluation/v1.2_failure_analysis.md`](v1.2_failure_analysis.md), an isolated, evaluation-only two-stage retrieval experiment was implemented and executed against the unchanged 80-case benchmark and 50-document corpus.

The experiment evaluated whether adding a neural cross-encoder reranker ([`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`](https://huggingface.co/cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2)) to re-rank the top 20 candidate passages from dense retrieval into a final top 5 could resolve hard-negative confusion and ranking errors without degrading production behavior.

### High-Level Outcomes

|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Overall MRR** | 0.7623 | **0.7762** | +0.0140 | **Failed Target** ($\ge 0.8500$) |
| **Hit Rate @ 1** | 0.6875 | **0.6750** | -0.0125 | Minor Regression (-1 net case) |
| **Hit Rate @ 3** | 0.8375 | **0.8625** | +0.0250 | Improvement (+2 net cases) |

| **Multi-Concept MRR** | 1.0000 | **0.9062** | -0.0938 | **Failed Safety Target** (Must be $1.0000$) |
| **Candidate Hit@20** | N/A | **0.9625** (77/80) | — | Excellent candidate recall |
| **P95 Latency** | 643.0 ms | **1,691.5 ms** | **+1,048.5 ms** | **Triggered Rejection Guardrail** ($> +300$ ms) |

### Key Conclusions

1. **Stage A Candidate Generation is Highly Effective:**  
   Dense retrieval with `top_k=20` captured the ground-truth document in **96.25% of cases** (77 of 80 cases). Only 3 hard-negative queries failed to place the relevant chunk in the top 20 candidates.
2. **Selective Domain Improvements:**  
   The cross-encoder successfully solved 12 difficult cases, dramatically boosting:
   - `boundary_handling` MRR: **0.5250 $\rightarrow$ 0.7604** ($+0.2354$)
   - `exact_terminology` MRR: **0.7937 $\rightarrow$ 0.9375** ($+0.1438$)
   - `cross_document` MRR: **0.7500 $\rightarrow$ 0.8750** ($+0.1250$)
   - Overall Hit Rate @ 5 reached **0.9250** (74 of 80 cases retrieved in top 5).
3. **Severe Off-Target Regressions:**  
   However, the model caused 13 regressions elsewhere:
   - Regressed `paraphrase` MRR from **0.9375 $\rightarrow$ 0.7854** ($-0.1521$).
   - Broke the multi-concept safety invariant (**1.0000 $\rightarrow$ 0.9062**), reordering 3 previously perfect queries down to rank 2.
   - Failed to solve hard-negative distractors (MRR flat at **0.4344** vs **0.4427**) because the general MS MARCO-trained cross-encoder actively preferred distractor documents that discussed queried technologies in depth.
4. **Latency Budget Violation:**  
   Reranking 20 candidate passages per query using ONNX Runtime on CPU added a median of **780.5 ms** (P95 of **926.0 ms**), driving end-to-end P95 latency to **1,691.5 ms** (+1,048.5 ms over control), decisively violating the 300 ms guardrail.

**Decision:** The treatment pipeline is **REJECTED** for production consideration.

## 2. Hypothesis

**Hypothesis:**  
A two-stage retrieval pipeline that retrieves candidate $top\_k = 20$ documents using Gemini dense embeddings and applies a local cross-encoder reranker (`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) to score query-candidate pairs jointly will capture fine-grained token-level cross-attention, elevating true KIP architectural documents over semantic distractors and increasing overall MRR from 0.7623 to $\ge 0.8500$.

**Assumptions Tested:**
1. Ground-truth documents displaced from top 5 in dense retrieval are present within candidate top 20.
2. Cross-encoder self-attention can distinguish genuine affirmative architectural statements from adversarial distractors.
3. ONNX Runtime CPU execution will run within acceptable latency bounds ($< 300$ ms overhead).

## 4. Model and Runtime Configuration

- **Model Identifier:** `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`
- **Model Source:** Official Hugging Face repository via `huggingface_hub`
- **Weights Format:** Official `onnx/model.onnx` (~85 MB)
- **Tokenizer Format:** Official `tokenizer.json` (~700 KB)
- **Runtime Engine:** `onnxruntime` v1.31.0
- **Execution Provider:** `CPUExecutionProvider`
- **Thread Configuration:** `inter_op_num_threads = 1`, `intra_op_num_threads = 4`
- **Graph Optimization:** `GraphOptimizationLevel.ORT_ENABLE_ALL`
- **Tokenization:** Dynamic batch padding to longest sequence in candidate set; truncation at 512 tokens
- **Score Representation:** Raw unnormalized logits from the single output linear head (higher score = more relevant). Not transformed to probabilities.
- **Dependency Impact:** **Zero new packages installed.** Leveraged existing `onnxruntime`, `tokenizers`, and `numpy` already present in the project `.venv`.
- **Cold-Start Overhead:** Model download and session initialization completed in **1,447.9 ms** (~1.45s) on initial load; subsequent queries run with zero initialization cost.

### Latency Findings

1. **Stage B Computational Cost:**  
   Scoring 20 passages using a 6-layer BERT cross-encoder with 512 max length on a modern CPU takes ~780 ms of sustained computation per query.
2. **End-to-End Latency Shift:**  
   The median retrieval latency increased from **538 ms** in the baseline to **1,316.2 ms** (+778.2 ms).
3. **P95 Latency Guardrail Violation:**  
   P95 latency increased from **643.0 ms** to **1,691.5 ms** (+1,048.5 ms). The pre-established guardrail specified rejection if P95 increased by more than 300 ms. This guardrail was decisively breached.

## 8. Failure-Case and Improvement Analysis

Across the 80 benchmark cases:
- **12 cases improved** ($RR_{treatment} > RR_{control}$)
- **13 cases regressed** ($RR_{treatment} < RR_{control}$)
- **55 cases remained identical**

### A. Improved Cases (Highlights)

The cross-encoder demonstrated remarkable power on queries with exact factual terminology or specific edge cases:

1. **`exp-007` (exact_terminology): $RR = 0.000 \rightarrow 1.000$ ($+1.000$)**  
   - *Query:* *"What language model drives generative responses in KIP production deployments?"*  
   - In control, target `kip_llm_gateway.txt` was ranked $>5$ by dense search. In treatment, it was candidate #6 in Stage A, and the cross-encoder elevated it directly to **Rank 1** (Score: $+3.81$).
2. **`exp-064` (hard_negative): $RR = 0.000 \rightarrow 1.000$ ($+1.000$)**  
   - *Query:* *"What entity coordinates vector transformations without introducing LangChain or LlamaIndex dependencies?"*  
   - Target `kip_embedding_manager.txt` was candidate #8; cross-encoder recognized the orchestration text and elevated it to **Rank 1** (Score: $+4.22$).
3. **`exp-070` (cross_document): $RR = 0.000 \rightarrow 1.000$ ($+1.000$)**  
   - *Query:* *"What language model providers can power answer generation and what models do they target?"*  
   - Target `kip_llm_gateway.txt` was candidate #7; cross-encoder promoted it to **Rank 1** (Score: $+4.05$).
4. **`exp-015` (exact_terminology): $RR = 0.200 \rightarrow 1.000$ ($+0.800$)**  
   - *Query:* *"What is the default temperature setting passed to LLM generation in KIP?"*  
   - Elevated `kip_llm_gateway.txt` from rank 5 to **Rank 1**.
5. **`exp-073` & `exp-075` (boundary_handling): $RR \rightarrow 1.000$**  
   - Elevated `kip_error_handling_protocols.txt` and `kip_empty_context_guard.txt` from ranks 5 and 3 to **Rank 1**.
6. **`exp-026` & `exp-077` Rescued from Zero:**  
   - Both rescued from complete misses ($RR=0.0$) into the top 3 ($RR=0.500$).

### B. Regressed Cases (Detailed Analysis)

Why did 13 cases regress?

1. **Negative Query Misinterpretation in Domain Hard Negatives:**  
   - In `exp-049` (hard_negative):  
     *Query:* *"What document chunking strategy does KIP actively implement in its production ingestion service?"*  
     Control had `kip_chunking_and_embeddings.txt` at **Rank 1** ($RR=1.000$).  
     The cross-encoder assigned a higher relevance score to `distractor_alternative_chunkers.txt` ($+6.84$) than to `kip_chunking_and_embeddings.txt` ($+4.11$), dropping the true target to **Rank 5** ($RR=0.200$). Because the distractor provides an in-depth taxonomy of chunking strategies, a generic model trained on MS MARCO judges it "more informative" about chunking strategies than KIP's concise documentation.
   - In `exp-060` (hard_negative):  
     *Query:* *"Does the active KIP retrieval pipeline include a second-stage cross-encoder reranker like Cohere Rerank?"*  
     Cross-encoder gave $+6.72$ to `distractor_cohere_reranking_pipeline.txt` and only $+1.42$ to `kip_retrieval_and_rag.txt`, pushing the target down from rank 2 to rank 4 ($RR = 0.500 \rightarrow 0.250$).
2. **Semantic Over-Focusing on Enterprise Jargon in Paraphrase Queries:**  
   - In `exp-022` (paraphrase):  
     *Query:* *"What prevents one enterprise tenant from reading another organization's confidential embeddings?"*  
     Target `kip_security_threat_model.txt` was at Rank 1 in control ($RR=1.0$).  
     The cross-encoder scored `distractor_oauth2_sso_authentication.txt` and `kip_multitenancy_architecture.txt` higher due to heavy token matches on "enterprise tenant", pushing the security threat model doc to **Rank 5** ($RR=0.200$).
   - In `exp-025` (paraphrase):  
     *Query:* *"How does the system ensure evaluation tests produce identical results across Windows and Linux machines?"*  
     Cross-encoder favored `distractor_kubernetes_cluster_orchestration.txt` over `kip_ground_truth_derivation.txt`, dropping the target to **Rank 5** ($RR = 1.0 \rightarrow 0.200$).
3. **Multi-Concept Inversion:**  
   - In `exp-035`, `exp-038`, and `exp-044`, queries combining multiple concepts were already ranked #1 by dense vector cosine similarity. The cross-encoder assigned marginally higher logits ($0.1$ to $0.3$ margin) to adjacent general architecture docs, displacing the targets to rank 2.

### C. Persistent Misses Attributed to Candidate Limitations (3 Cases)

Three cases failed to retrieve a relevant document in both Stage A and Stage B ($CandidateHit@20 = False$):
- **`exp-053` (hard_negative):** *"What caching technology is deployed in KIP v1.1..."*  
  Target `kip_empty_context_guard.txt` was absent from the top 20 dense candidates (ranked #24). The reranker cannot score candidates it never receives.
- **`exp-055` (hard_negative):** *"What autonomous multi-step reasoning framework like LangGraph or CrewAI powers KIP workflows?"*  
  Target `kip_rag_service_orchestration.txt` was absent from the top 20 candidates.
- **`exp-056` (hard_negative):** *"Does KIP store knowledge relationships in a Neo4j property graph database for GraphRAG?"*  
  Target `kip_vector_store_adapters.txt` was absent from the top 20 candidates.

*Finding:* 100% of cases where the candidate set contained the ground truth were evaluated by the reranker; the 3 remaining misses are strictly bounded by first-stage dense candidate recall.

## 10. Success Criteria Evaluation & Decision

| Criterion | Target | Measured | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Target** | Overall MRR $\ge 0.8500$ | **0.7762** ($+0.0140$) | **FAILED** |
| **Secondary Target** | Hard Negative MRR $\ge 0.6500$ | **0.4344** ($-0.0083$) | **FAILED** |
| **Safety Target** | Multi-Concept MRR $= 1.0000$ | **0.9062** ($-0.0938$) | **FAILED** |
| **Latency Guardrail** | P95 latency increase $\le 300$ ms | **+1,048.5 ms** | **TRIGGERED** |

### Decision

**REJECT `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2` as a drop-in reranker for KIP.**

### Justification
1. **Ineffective against domain hard negatives:** Because MS MARCO pre-training optimizes for general passage relevance rather than factual attribution/negation verification, the cross-encoder strongly rewards distractor passages that contain high lexical density of the negative technologies.
2. **Collateral damage on paraphrase and multi-concept:** Introducing this cross-encoder degraded performance on queries where pure dense retrieval was already optimal, producing 13 regressions against 12 improvements.
3. **Severe latency penalty:** An increase of +1,048.5 ms at P95 violates real-time user experience standards for zero-cost RAG applications.

## 11. Recommendations for Next Research Iteration

1. **Investigate Hybrid Sparse/Dense Search (BM25 + Qdrant Dense Fusion):**  
   Instead of an expensive neural cross-encoder, evaluate Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) between lexical BM25 and dense Gemini embeddings. This can improve exact terminology matching with zero CPU inference overhead.
2. **Domain-Specific or Fine-Tuned Cross-Encoder:**  
   If reranking is pursued in v1.3+, the reranker must be fine-tuned or prompt-instructed (e.g., via BAAI/bge-reranker-large or lightweight LLM reranking) to recognize negated concepts (*"KIP does NOT use X"*).
3. **Candidate Pruning for Latency:**  
   If neural reranking is reconsidered, reduce Stage A candidate pool from 20 to 10 to cut reranking latency by ~50%.
