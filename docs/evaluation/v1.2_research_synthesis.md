## 1. Executive Summary

The Knowledge Intelligence Platform (KIP) v1.2 development cycle transitioned the repository from speculative feature additions into a disciplined, hypothesis-driven empirical research program following the 14-step research lifecycle.

Across v1.2, **five controlled empirical experiments** were formulated, implemented, and benchmarked against frozen evaluation datasets:
1. **Asymmetric Embedding Input Formatting (`EXP-V12-INPUT-FORMAT-001`)**
2. **Cross-Encoder Reranking (`EXP-V12-CROSS-ENCODER-001`)**
3. **Hybrid BM25 + Dense Retrieval with RRF (`EXP-V12-HYBRID-RETRIEVAL-001`)**
4. **Sentence-Boundary Chunking (`EXP-V12-SENTENCE-CHUNKING-001`)**
5. **Context Optimization (`EXP-V12-CONTEXT-OPTIMIZATION-001`)**

**Overall Research Finding:** None of the five candidate retrieval techniques satisfied the preregistered primary target of Overall Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) $\ge 0.8200$ without violating latency budgets or safety guardrails. Under strict research integrity:
- Every candidate was rejected or deemed neutral under its preregistered decision criteria.
- **Dense whole-document retrieval (Overall MRR 0.7623, Hit@5 0.8750) is retained as the stable production retrieval default.**
- Production retrieval code under `backend/app/` remains completely unmodified by the rejected experimental treatments.

---

## 2. Research Question and Hypotheses

### Primary Research Question
*Can retrieval quality across technical documentation be improved from the dense retrieval baseline to Overall MRR $\ge 0.8200$ while maintaining interactive query latency ($< 1,000$ ms P95 end-to-end) and preserving safety invariants on multi-concept queries?*

### Preregistered Targets & Guardrails
- **Primary Retrieval Target:** Overall MRR $\ge 0.8200$ across the 80-case expanded benchmark.
- **Latency Guardrail:** End-to-end P95 retrieval latency must not increase by more than $+300$ ms over the baseline.
- **Safety Invariant:** `multi_concept` MRR must remain strictly $1.0000$ (all ground-truth documents retrieved at rank 1).
- **Hard-Negative Discrimination:** Hard-negative category MRR target $\ge 0.6500$.

---

## 3. Dataset and Evaluation Protocol

### 3.1 Datasets
1. **Expanded Reference Corpus (`benchmarks/corpus_v1_2_expanded/`):**
   - 50 technical plain-text documents (`.txt`, strictly Unix LF line endings).
   - Mean document size: ~812 characters (~120 tokens). Documents represent self-contained architectural, operational, and algorithmic briefs.
   - SHA-256 corpus hash: `287b979cd88c93e5f64a8d3104df7756c6efb8e1a30132b69649c52e70deb17d`.
2. **Expanded Benchmark Dataset (`benchmarks/kip_v1_2_expanded_benchmark.json`):**
   - 80 labelled evaluation cases categorized into 6 distinct challenges:
     - `exact_terminology` (16 cases): Queries targeting specific acronyms, configuration flags, or error codes.
     - `paraphrase` (16 cases): Conceptual questions phrased using semantic synonyms without keyword overlap.
     - `hard_negative` (16 cases): Adversarial queries designed to trigger keyword-dense distractor documents.
     - `multi_concept` (16 cases): Compound technical queries requiring joint understanding of multiple components.
     - `boundary_handling` (8 cases): Queries addressing definitions near section or sentence transitions.
     - `cross_document` (8 cases): Comparative queries touching multiple related concepts.
   - SHA-256 dataset hash: `a03cf256094f574ea36ee295f0d4b862dca48135f30f3082c75b0af561fbfe1b`.
3. **Pilot Benchmark Dataset (`benchmarks/kip_v1_2_benchmark.json`):**
   - 25 cases evaluated over an 18-document pilot corpus (`benchmarks/corpus_v1_2/`), used exclusively for the initial input-formatting pilot (`EXP-V12-INPUT-FORMAT-001`).

### 3.2 Relevance Semantics
- Evaluated at top-k = 5 (`retrieval_top_k = 5`).
- A case is scored as a hit if any `expected_document_ids` (or `expected_chunk_ids`) appears in the top-k retrieved list.
- Reciprocal rank is computed as $1 / \text{rank}$ for the first relevant item, or $0.0$ if no relevant item appears within top-k.
- Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) and Hit@K are computed strictly over labelled cases.

### 3.3 Known Dataset Limitations
- **Corpus Scale:** 50 technical documents is a compact, curated corpus. While ideal for controlled diagnostic error analysis, performance patterns may shift on multi-gigabyte corpora with millions of chunks.
- **Document Length:** Average document length (~800 characters) means single whole-document chunks fit entirely within the embedding model context window (768 dimensions), giving whole-document retrieval a natural contextual advantage over fragmented sub-chunks.

## 5. Individual Experiment Findings

### 5.1 Embedding Input Formatting (`EXP-V12-INPUT-FORMAT-001`)
- **What Changed:** Evaluated whether prepending asymmetric prefix tokens (`task: question answering | query: ...` and `title: none | text: ...`) aligns embedding spaces better than raw text.
- **What Was Held Constant:** Corpus (18 pilot docs), embedding model (`gemini-embedding-2`), vector store (Qdrant).
- **What Improved:** 1 case (`exp-012`) improved from rank 2 to rank 1.
- **What Regressed:** 1 case (`exp-008`) regressed from rank 1 to rank 2.
- **Outcome:** MRR remained exactly identical at **0.9133 vs 0.9133** ($\Delta = +0.0000$). 23 of 25 cases (92%) had identical ranks.
- **Decision:** **REJECT (Neutral)**. Raw text retained to prevent token overhead and artificial API coupling.

### 5.2 Cross-Encoder Reranking (`EXP-V12-CROSS-ENCODER-001`)
- **What Changed:** Replaced single-stage dense retrieval with a two-stage pipeline: top-20 candidates retrieved by Gemini embeddings, re-ranked by `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2` ONNX to top-5.
- **What Was Held Constant:** 50-document corpus, 80-case expanded benchmark, candidate generation pool.
- **What Improved:** Resolved 12 difficult cases; `boundary_handling` MRR rose 0.5250 $\rightarrow$ 0.7604 (+0.2354); Hit@5 reached **0.9250**.
- **What Regressed:** 
  - `multi_concept` MRR dropped from **1.0000 to 0.9062** (safety invariant violated).
  - `paraphrase` MRR dropped from **0.9375 to 0.7854** (-0.1521).
  - Added ~791 ms median reranking latency, pushing end-to-end P95 latency to **1,691.5 ms** (+1,048.5 ms overhead), violating the +300 ms guardrail.
- **Decision:** **REJECT**. Latency explosion and safety regression.

### 5.3 Hybrid BM25 + Dense Retrieval (`EXP-V12-HYBRID-RETRIEVAL-001`)
- **What Changed:** Fused top-20 dense candidates with top-20 BM25Okapi lexical candidates using Reciprocal Rank Fusion ($k=60$) to return top-5.
- **What Was Held Constant:** 50-document corpus, 80-case expanded benchmark.
- **What Improved:** `exact_terminology` Hit@1 rose from 0.6875 $\rightarrow$ 0.7500; `cross_document` MRR rose from 0.7500 $\rightarrow$ 0.8125.
- **What Regressed:** Overall MRR dropped sharply from **0.7623 to 0.7094** (-0.0529). `paraphrase` MRR collapsed from **0.9375 to 0.6979** (-0.2396) because distractor documents with incidental keyword overlap overwhelmed semantic signals.
- **Decision:** **REJECT**. Severe retrieval quality regression on conceptual queries.

### 5.4 Sentence-Boundary Chunking (`EXP-V12-SENTENCE-CHUNKING-001`)
- **What Changed:** Segmented documents into 173 sentence-bounded chunks (target 400 chars, min 150 chars, 1 sentence overlap, ~3.46 chunks/doc) instead of 50 whole-document chunks.
- **What Was Held Constant:** 50-document corpus, 80-case expanded benchmark.
- **What Improved:** `boundary_handling` MRR rose from 0.5250 $\rightarrow$ 0.6917; `exact_terminology` Hit@1 rose from 0.6875 $\rightarrow$ 0.8125. Hit@5 reached 0.9125.
- **What Regressed:** Semantic fragmentation impaired thematic queries: `paraphrase` MRR dropped 0.9375 $\rightarrow$ 0.8125; `hard_negative` dropped 0.4427 $\rightarrow$ 0.3563; `multi_concept` dropped 1.0000 $\rightarrow$ 0.9688 (safety violation on `exp-038`). Dedup MRR was **0.7471** (-0.0152 vs control 0.7623).
- **Decision:** **REJECT**. Net negative on holistic thematic retrieval.

### 5.5 Context Optimization (`EXP-V12-CONTEXT-OPTIMIZATION-001`)
- **What Changed:** Replaced static uncompressed top-5 context (~4,533 chars) with dynamic budget allocation (2,500 chars), document deduplication, and extractive keyword-salience compression.
- **What Was Held Constant:** Top-5 candidate retrieval inputs, 50-document corpus, 80-case benchmark.
- **What Improved:** Reduced admitted prompt context by **45.96%** (2,449 characters) with 0.042 ms P95 CPU overhead. High-rank safety was preserved (`multi_concept` 1.0000, `paraphrase` 0.9375, `cross_document` 0.7500).
- **What Regressed:** Fixed character ceiling truncated true relevant evidence located at rank 4 or 5 in 3 cases (`exp-015`, `exp-050`, `exp-073`), lowering Hit@5 from 0.8750 $\rightarrow$ 0.8375 and MRR from 0.7623 $\rightarrow$ **0.7542**.
- **Decision:** **REJECT** for standalone retrieval modification; failed target MRR $\ge 0.8200$.

## 7. Production Decision

In accordance with strict empirical engineering principles, **no candidate technique will be promoted to production**.

- **Production Retrieval Baseline Retained:** Dense whole-document retrieval using Google Gemini embeddings (`gemini-embedding-2`, 768 dimensions) with Qdrant vector storage (`top_k = 5`).
- **Production Code Unchanged:** `backend/app/services/retrieval/` and `backend/app/services/chunking/` remain free of experimental code branches, reranker dependencies, or hybrid fusion logic.

## 8. Future Architectural Implications: Hierarchical Retrieval (v1.3+ Hypothesis)

The unified takeaway across the v1.2 empirical program is that chunking, retrieval granularity, and context assembly cannot be solved in isolation:
- Small chunks provide fine-grained boundary matching but lack thematic context.
- Large chunks preserve thematic context but cause boundary clipping and distractor overlap.
- Tight prompt budgets save LLM tokens but penalize deep-rank recall.

**Proposed Future Hypothesis for v1.3/v2.0:**  
**Hierarchical (Parent-Child) Indexing:**
- **Index/Search Granularity (Child):** Small sentence-level chunks (~250–350 chars) indexed for high-precision semantic matching.
- **Context/Assembly Granularity (Parent):** When a child chunk matches, its parent document or enclosing context section (~800–1200 chars) is fetched and provided to the context builder.

*Status: This is an unverified research hypothesis to be evaluated under a future milestone lifecycle, not a proven feature.*
